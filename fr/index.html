Les défis de l'expansion au Vietnam

L'expansion au Vietnam peut sembler compliquée : la réglementation, les canaux de distribution, la gestion des fournisseurs, le recrutement de talents et la localisation de marque exigent tous du temps et de l'expertise. Sans les bons conseils, les entreprises risquent de gaspiller des ressources et de manquer des opportunités.

Pourquoi investir au Vietnam maintenant ?

Le Vietnam est l'un des marchés à la croissance la plus rapide au monde pour les FMCG, le commerce de détail et les produits de consommation. Mais le succès sur le marché demande plus que de l'ambition. Vous avez besoin d'une expertise locale, de partenaires fiables et d'une stratégie agile. C'est exactement ce que nous offrons.

Questions fréquemment posées

What does Provigood do? +

Provigood is a Ho Chi Minh City–based consulting and trading company, helping food, beverage, FMCG and hospitality companies grow in, from and to Vietnam. We deliver integrated services across market entry, business development, team empowerment, and corporate services.

How do I enter the Vietnamese food market? +

Our 4-step approach: understand the market, accelerate growth, optimize operations, and scale sustainably. We handle research, legal setup, partner identification, branding and operations, tailored to your sector and timeline.

How do I become a Provigood partner? +

We welcome experts and firms in legal, marketing, logistics, sourcing, HR, training, finance and corporate services. Apply via our partner page, we'll set up an initial discovery call and explore mutual fit.

Can Provigood import and distribute my products in Vietnam? +

Subject to confirmation of the product category, Provigood is licensed to import, wholesale, distribute and, where applicable, retail a wide range of products, including most food and fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG).

From a technical and regulatory standpoint, Provigood can support the full process required to register your products in Vietnam, as well as their importation, warehousing and distribution.

These activities are generally carried out on behalf of our customers and at their own cost and risk, depending on the scope and nature of the services required.
