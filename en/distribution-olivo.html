Sustainable delivery solution for perishable goods

Discover Olivo Cold Logistics passive cold solutions

Olivo Cold Logistics insulated containers enable the safe transportation of temperature-controlled goods for up to 12 hours (BOX) and 72 hours (BAC, ROLL) without mechanical refrigeration or cold trucks. Olivo Cold Logistics insulated containers leverage various sources of cold such as eutectic plates or cryogenic cold to replace mechanical cooling.

Key benefits

1Safety

  • Protects the integrity and quality of temperature-sensitive products throughout the cold chain.
  • Maintains required temperatures for up to 72 hours (BAC, ROLL) or 12 hours (BOX) without mechanical refrigeration.
  • Manufactured from food-grade materials suitable for food and pharmaceutical applications.
  • Helps prevent cross-contamination through safe product segregation.

2Compliance

  • Improves product quality and regulatory compliance.
  • OLIVO BAC and ROLL containers are certified A.T.P. (United Nations, Agreement for Transport of Perishables) by CEMAFROID.
  • Supports compliance with Vietnam's Law on Food Safety No. 55/2010/QH12 and its latest implementing regulation, Decree No. 46/2026/ND-CP.
  • Facilitates the implementation of HACCP, ISO 22000 and GDP (Good Distribution Practices) by ensuring reliable temperature control throughout the cold chain.

3Durability

  • Olivo insulated containers are designed for intensive daily professional use. They require low maintenance and offer outstanding long-term reliability.
  • 12-year average service life for BAC and ROLL.
  • 6-year average service life for BOX.
  • Eutectic plates have an unlimited service life under normal daily operating conditions.

4Sustainability

  • Supports your company's ESG and sustainability objectives.
  • Reduces dependence on refrigerated vehicles and diesel consumption. Lowers CO2 emissions.
  • Olivo BOX are manufactured from 100% recyclable EPP (Expanded Polypropylene).
  • Replaces single-use polystyrene packaging with durable, reusable containers.

5Cost effectiveness

  • Typical Return on Investment (ROI) of less than 24 months.
  • Transport products requiring different temperature ranges in the same vehicle.
  • Reduce the number of deliveries and optimize vehicle utilization.
  • Replace refrigerated trucks with standard ambient vehicles in many operations.
  • Reduce transportation costs by 30% on average.
  • Benefit from one of the market's lowest Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) and Cost per Use (CpU).

Sustainability & ESG impact

Olivo Cold Logistics insulated containers help businesses achieve CSR and ESG compliance by:

  1. Securing the cold chain at all times during picking, loading, and unloading, increasing food safety and reducing food waste.
  2. Reducing delivery trips by combining several temperatures of goods in one vehicle, cutting fuel consumption and CO2 emissions while improving urban traffic flow, lowering energy consumption and delivery costs.
  3. Replacing single-use polystyrene foam boxes with reusable, durable, and recyclable alternatives, reducing single-use plastic waste.

Frequently asked questions, Olivo Cold Logistics

What is Olivo Cold Logistics and how does it work? +

Founded in 1956, Olivo Cold Logistics is France's leading manufacturer of passive temperature-controlled containers. Represented by Provigood in Vietnam and Cambodia, Olivo solutions maintain the required temperatures without mechanical refrigeration. Using eutectic plates or cryogenic cooling, they preserve cold chain integrity for up to 12 hours (BOX) and up to 72 hours (BAC and ROLL). ATP-certified for the transport of perishable goods, Olivo containers have a service life exceeding 12 years and enable multi-temperature deliveries in a single dry truck or motorbike, significantly reducing fuel consumption, CO2 emissions, and distribution costs.

What formats does Olivo offer? +

Olivo offers three ranges of insulated passive containers—BOX, BAC, and ROLL—with capacities from 30L to 1,400L to suit every transport requirement. Designed for use on motorbikes, cars, vans, and trucks, they provide safe and reliable temperature-controlled transport for both food and pharmaceutical products. Combined with eutectic plates or cryogenic cooling, Olivo solutions maintain chilled or frozen temperatures for transport durations of up to 72 hours, without mechanical refrigeration. Contact us for a detailed catalog and quote.

Where and by whom is Olivo used? +

Olivo solutions are used in more than 50 countries by companies operating in the food, pharmaceutical, healthcare, retail, catering, e-commerce, and logistics sectors. They are trusted by manufacturers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, hospitals, laboratories, pharmacies, and third-party logistics providers (3PLs) for safe, reliable, and sustainable temperature-controlled transport. Suitable for both urban last-mile deliveries and long-distance distribution, Olivo containers perform wherever the cold chain must be maintained without mechanical refrigeration.
