Le Caïffa

A Heritage of Coffee. Reimagined for Today.

Since 1890, Le Caïffa has carried the spirit of Vietnamese coffee into a new generation. From exceptional Vietnamese origins to carefully crafted specialty roasts, Le Caïffa combines heritage, craftsmanship and modern capsule technology to create a distinctive coffee experience at home, in hospitality and in premium professional environments.

Frequently asked questions, Le Caïffa coffee capsules

Are Le Caïffa coffee capsules compatible with my coffee machine? +

Yes, most likely. Le Caïffa coffee capsules exist under different forms and shapes:

  • 6 g pods compatible with the Nespresso system,
  • 10 g pods compatible with the Le Caïffa system,
  • 10 g, 12 g or 14 g paper pods compatible with any ground-coffee espresso system.

Le Caïffa roasted beans are also available in 100 g gift bags.

If you do not have a coffee machine yet, we can provide one to you as well.

Are Le Caïffa coffee capsules truly compostable? +

Yes. Le Caïffa coffee capsules are fully compostable and made in Vietnam. The Arabica and Robusta beans are sourced primarily from Cầu Đất, Đà Lạt and Buôn Ma Thuột provinces, then masterfully ground, roasted and packed in Ho Chi Minh City. The capsules biodegrade naturally and are an eco-friendly alternative to aluminum or plastic capsules.

Can we white-label or co-brand Le Caïffa coffee capsules? +

Yes. We offer co-branded and private-label opportunities for B2B partners, subject to volume. We can also supply compatible capsule machines for hospitality and corporate environments and provide sustainability storytelling support: content, certifications and packaging copy. Contact us to discuss volumes and terms.

Can I become a sub-distributor of Le Caïffa coffee capsules in my country? +

Yes, Provigood is open to sub-distribution partnerships across Vietnam, Southeast Asia and worldwide. We have existing distribution arrangements in Cambodia and we are actively exploring partnerships in other markets. Reach out via our partner page if you have an established food, hospitality or retail network in your country.
